SHORT HOP — MATCHING LOGIC SIMPLIFICATION (FINAL V1 DRIVER-FIRST MODEL)

This update simplifies and corrects the matching system.

Goal:  
Make matching strict, predictable, and simple while allowing small natural pickup/drop-off adjustments.

---

# 🚨 IMPORTANT RULES

- Do NOT redesign UI  
- Do NOT change layout or navigation  
- Do NOT introduce new features  
- ONLY update matching logic and related conditions  

---

# 🎯 CORE PRINCIPLE

Drivers are matched with riders going in the same direction.

Drivers should NOT significantly change their route.

However, small automatic adjustments (short turns or pull-offs) are allowed.

---

# 🚫 REMOVE THESE LOGICS COMPLETELY

Remove or disable:

- walking distance preferences  
- “willing to walk”  
- manual pickup negotiation  
- complex detour settings  
- user-controlled route adjustments  
- multi-step routing logic  

---

# 🧠 FINAL MATCHING LOGIC

A match ONLY happens if ALL conditions below are true:

---

## 1. TIME MATCH

- abs(driver.departure_time - hopper.departure_time) ≤ 30 minutes  

Else → NO MATCH

---

## 2. DESTINATION ALIGNMENT

- hopper destination must be ALONG the driver’s route  
- hopper destination must NOT be ahead beyond driver’s destination  

Else → NO MATCH

---

## 3. PICKUP POSITION (NO FORWARD PICKUPS)

- hopper pickup must be:
  - on the route OR  
  - slightly off the route (within tolerance)  
- hopper must NOT be ahead of the driver’s current route direction  

Else → NO MATCH

---

## 4. MICRO-ADJUSTMENT RANGE (IMPORTANT)

Allow small automatic route deviation ONLY within:

- max 0.5 miles from route  

This applies to:
- pickup  
- drop-off  

Behavior:
- driver can briefly leave route  
- pick up or drop off  
- immediately return to route  

No large deviation allowed.

---

## 5. DROPOFF RULE (NO STRANDING)

- hopper must be dropped:
  - directly on their route  
  OR  
  - very close to their destination (within 0.5 miles)

DO NOT allow:
- dropping hopper far away requiring long walking  

---

## 6. SEAT AVAILABILITY

- driver.available_seats ≥ hopper.seats_needed  

Else → NO MATCH

---

# ✅ FINAL CONDITION

Only if ALL conditions pass → MATCH  
Otherwise → NO MATCH

---
