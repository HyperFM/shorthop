SHORT HOP FEATURE UPDATE
Dynamic Pricing Slider + Recommended Pricing System

OBJECTIVE
Implement a user-controlled pricing system inside the Tailor Experience section that allows both Drivers and Hoppers to set their preferred price per 0.5 mile using a slider.

The system should:
• Default to a recommended price of $1.20 per 0.5 mile
• Allow pricing from FREE up to a configurable maximum
• Provide real-time guidance messaging based on selected price
• Maintain ShortHop’s percentage-based platform fee
• Allow drivers to offer free rides to specific people or followers


----------------------------------------------------

UI LOCATION

Settings
→ Tailor Experience

Existing toggle already present:
Driver | Hopper

Below the role selection add a new section:

Pricing Preferences


----------------------------------------------------

PRICING SLIDER

Create a horizontal slider labeled:

Price Per 0.5 Mile

Slider range:
$0.00 (FREE) → $5.00

Default position:
$1.20 per 0.5 mile

Display example:

FREE ─────────●──────── $5.00
               $1.20

Below the slider show live value:

Your Price: $1.20 per 0.5 mile


----------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDED PRICING GUIDANCE

Display dynamic messaging depending on the selected price.

Sweet Spot Range
$0.90 – $1.40

Message:
Recommended

Most ShortHop riders and drivers match fastest in this range.

$1.20 per half mile is the most common price and usually leads to the quickest ride matches.


----------------------------------------------------

LOW PRICE RANGE

$0.00 – $0.80

Message:

Low Price

Lower prices can help you match faster and help more people get where they need to go.
Many drivers choose this range when they are already headed the same direction.


----------------------------------------------------

FREE PRICE

$0.00

Message:

Free Ride

You are offering rides for free.
This is great for helping others or giving rides to friends and regular riders.


----------------------------------------------------

HIGH PRICE RANGE

Above $1.75

Message:

Higher Price

This price is higher than most ShortHop rides.

You may receive fewer ride matches unless riders nearby are willing to pay this rate.

----------------------------------------------------

MATCHING LOGIC

Drivers set:
minimum price per 0.5 mile

Riders set:
maximum price per 0.5 mile

Matching rule:

IF rider_price >= driver_price
MATCH


Example:

Driver minimum: $0.80
Rider max: $1.20

Match accepted.

UX GOAL

Users should feel like they are customizing their ride economy rather than being forced into fixed pricing.

The system should gently guide users toward the recommended price of about $1 per half mile while still allowing full freedom.
