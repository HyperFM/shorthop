ShortHop Update – Early Network Transparency + Growth Notifications + Founding Members

Goal:
Implement a transparent onboarding system, founding member rewards, and community growth notifications so early users understand they are part of building the ShortHop network in Lexington.

The system should encourage users to stay engaged, invite friends, and watch the network grow.

---

1. New User Welcome Notification

When a user first installs and creates an account, show a welcome card with the following message.

Display this as a clean modal card inside the app.

Welcome to ShortHop 🛞

Hey Hopper or Driver!

You’re one of the early people helping bring something new to life in Lexington.

Apps that connect people — like Uber or Airbnb — didn’t grow overnight. They started with small communities of people who believed in the idea.

ShortHop is starting right here in Lexington, and together we’re building a new way to move around the city.

Right now rides may not appear instantly yet — but every new Hopper and Driver brings us closer to that moment where a ride is just minutes away.

And the best part?

You’re part of the beginning.

Invite friends, tell family, and help grow the network. Every new user helps the city move a little more together.

We’ll keep you updated as our community grows.

Shared routes. Real connections.

– SHORT HOP

---

2. Founding Hoppers & Founding Drivers

Reward the earliest community members.

The first:

20 Hoppers  
20 Drivers

should automatically receive:

Founding Hopper or Founding Driver status.

Benefits:

• Lifetime FlexHop / PowerHop access  
• Special profile badge: "Founding Hopper" or "Founding Driver"  
• Early supporter recognition in the system

Example badge:

🛞 Founding Hopper

These users helped start the ShortHop network and should always retain this status.

---

3. Invite Friends Button

Below the welcome message include two buttons:

Invite Friends  
Turn On Growth Notifications

Invite Friends should open the phone share sheet so users can send the app to friends through:

• text message  
• social media  
• messenger apps  
• email

Encourage inviting 10–15 friends to help the network grow.

Example invite text:

"Join me on ShortHop — a new way for people in Lexington to share rides along their routes. Shared routes. Real connections."

---

4. Growth Notifications System

Implement a community growth notification system that updates users as the network grows.

Example notifications:

"2 new Hoppers joined this week!"

"Welcome new Drivers to the network."

"ShortHop now has 15 users in Lexington."

"Lexington is growing — more routes are becoming active."

"50 Hoppers have joined ShortHop!"

"The network is growing. Keep hopping!"

---

5. Lexington Network Milestones

Track total Lexington users and send milestone notifications.

Example milestones:

10 users  
25 users  
50 users  
100 users  
250 users  
500 users  
1,000 users  
2,000 users  
3,000 users  
5,000 users

When milestones occur send notifications like:

"ShortHop just reached 500 users in Lexington."

"1,000 Hoppers and Drivers are now part of ShortHop!"

These notifications should automatically stop once Lexington reaches approximately 5,000 users.

After this point the network should be active enough that growth updates are no longer needed.

---

6. Community Progress Indicator

Add a small section in the app showing network growth.

Example:

Lexington ShortHop Network

Drivers: 132  
Hoppers: 417  

Total Community: 549

Next milestone: 1,000 users

This helps early users feel involved in building the network.

---

7. Notification Limits

Limit growth notifications to prevent spam.

Maximum recommended frequency:

1–2 notifications per week.

Users can disable growth notifications anytime in settings.

---

8. Purpose of This Feature

ShortHop is a network-based platform.

Early transparency helps users understand that the community is growing together and encourages them to help expand the network by inviting others.

This builds trust and engagement during the early growth stage.
