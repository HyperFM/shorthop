1. What Short Hop Is

Short Hop is a movement-first transportation platform that helps people travel from point A to point B using the most efficient combination of walking and rides.

Instead of assuming every trip requires a full ride, Short Hop allows users to move forward in stages.

Users can:

• walk when possible
• advance along routes drivers are already traveling
• request small detours when needed
• take a direct ride when necessary

The entire journey happens inside one app.

Short Hop never redirects users to outside ride platforms.

⸻

2. The Problem

Most trips don’t require a full ride.

People usually just need help with the last mile.

Common situations:

• the destination is close but walking takes too long
• bus routes don’t align with where someone needs to go
• traditional rideshare services charge too much for short trips
• someone is running slightly late

Current transportation apps force an all-or-nothing decision.

Either walk the entire way or pay for a full ride.

Short Hop fills the gap between those two options.

⸻

3. Core Philosophy

Short Hop is built around a simple progression.

1️⃣ Walk when possible
2️⃣ Advance along routes already in motion
3️⃣ Request flexible help if needed
4️⃣ Use Power Hop when you need to go directly

Transportation should support momentum, not replace it.

⸻

7. Movement Options

When a user enters a destination, Short Hop calculates the best movement strategy.

The app presents the following options.

⸻

🟢 Walk

Encouraged when the destination is nearby.

Display includes:

• distance
• estimated time
• optional health stats

Navigation features:

• GPS path
• animated walking indicator
• voice guidance

Cost: $0

⸻

🟡 Short Hop (Advance)

Short Hop connects walkers with drivers already traveling along the same path.

Drivers do not change their route.

They simply advance the walker forward.

Example:

Driver route
Home → Downtown

Walker destination
2 miles further along that direction.

Driver advances the walker part of the way.

Cost: $1–$3

Benefits:

• extremely low cost
• minimal driver disruption
• efficient movement

⸻

🟠 Flex Hop (Optional Detours)

Some drivers may enable Flex Hop.

These drivers allow small detours when helpful.

Example:

Driver route
Home → Downtown

Walker destination
1 mile past the route.

Driver prompt:

“Flex Hop request available — continue 0.8 miles past route.”

Driver options:

• Accept detour
• Continue normal route

Drivers can configure:

• maximum detour distance
• maximum extra time
• detours on/off

Pricing adjusts dynamically.

⸻

✨ POWER HOP (Unlimited Direct Ride)

Power Hop is the premium direct ride option inside Short Hop.

It allows a rider to travel directly from their starting point to their destination without relying on route overlap.

This option replaces traditional rideshare services within the Short Hop ecosystem.

Power Hop ensures that users never need to leave the app to complete their trip.

Power Hop UI Design

Power Hop appears as a special highlighted option within the movement choices.

Visual design:

• glowing green and orange ring surrounding the option
• slightly larger visual presence than other options
• animated glow to signal a premium feature

Slogan displayed beneath the option:

“Reach for the Sky.”

This option represents the most powerful movement choice available inside Short Hop.

⸻

Updated Movement Ladder

Users progress through movement options like this:

🟢 Walk
🟡 Short Hop
🟠 Flex Hop
✨ Power Hop

Each level provides more assistance and faster movement.

18. Strategic Position (Updated)

Traditional rideshare platforms focus only on full rides.

Short Hop focuses on progressive movement.

Walk → Hop → Flex → Power.

By offering Power Hop inside the platform, Short Hop allows users to complete any journey without leaving the app.

This creates a complete mobility ecosystem.

Short Hop assumes capability.

The platform provides a clean, unified experience where users move from A to B without leaving the app.

Transportation should feel:

• simple

• empowering

Short Hop helps people move forward — one hop at a time.
