MATCHING SYSTEM (PLAIN LOGIC)

Core Goal

Match riders (“hoppers”) with drivers who are already going their way, in a way that feels:
- effortless  
- natural  
- not forced  

---

SYSTEM STRUCTURE

There are always two live lists:

Drivers List

Each driver has:
- current location  
- destination  
- route (the path they’re already taking)  
- number of available seats  
- preference:
  - One Rider Only  
  - Maximize Seats  

---

Hoppers List

Each hopper has:
- pickup location  
- dropoff location  

---

HOW MATCHING WORKS (STEP BY STEP)

1. Compare routes  

For every hopper, the system checks every driver and asks:

“Is this hopper basically on the way?”

---

2. A match is ONLY valid if ALL are true:

Pickup is close to the driver’s route  
- within 0.5 miles  

Dropoff is close to the driver’s route  
- within 0.5 miles  

Hopper is going the same direction  
- not opposite or backwards  

Dropoff is not too far past the driver’s destination  
- cannot exceed 0.5 miles beyond destination  

Driver has available seats  

---

If ANY of these fail:

Do not match  
(even if it is the only driver available)

---

MATCH PRIORITY (IMPORTANT)

The system does not match randomly.

It prioritizes:

1. Drivers who want to “Maximize Seats”  
   - fill these cars first  

2. Drivers who want “One Rider Only”  
   - match them after  

---

HOW ASSIGNMENT WORKS

For “Maximize Seats” drivers:
- Find all valid hoppers along their route  
- Sort them by best fit (closest to route)  
- Fill seats one by one until:
  - seats are full  
  - or no more good matches  

---

For “One Rider Only” drivers:
- Find the single best hopper  
- Assign only that one  
- Stop  

---

WHAT “BEST MATCH” MEANS

A better match is:
- closer pickup to route  
- closer dropoff to route  

The system prefers:
hoppers that require the least deviation  

---

SEAT LOGIC

Every time a hopper is matched:
- reduce driver’s available seats  

When seats reach 0:
- driver is removed from the matching pool  

---

IMPORTANT BEHAVIOR RULES

Do not match just because someone is available  

Do not send drivers across town  

Do not go far past destination  

Do not create awkward detours  

---

WHAT THE SYSTEM SHOULD FEEL LIKE

To the user, it should feel like:

“They were already going my way.”

Not:

“The driver went out of their way for me.”

---

MATCHING TIMING

Instead of instant matching:
- The system runs in short cycles (every few seconds)  
- It evaluates all drivers and all hoppers together  
- It assigns matches intelligently  

This allows:
- better seat filling  
- smarter grouping  
- less randomness  

---

BIG PICTURE

This is not a taxi system.

This is a shared route alignment system.

Drivers do not change their plan.  
Riders fit into existing movement.
