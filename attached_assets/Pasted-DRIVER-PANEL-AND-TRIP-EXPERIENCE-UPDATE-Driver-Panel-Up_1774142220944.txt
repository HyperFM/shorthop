DRIVER PANEL AND TRIP EXPERIENCE UPDATE

Driver Panel Update

Add a toggle option in the driver panel with two clear choices:
- One Rider Only  
- Maximize Seats  

This toggle controls how the matching system assigns hoppers to the driver, without changing any existing matching logic.

---

Driver Navigation Experience

Once a driver is matched with a hopper, the app should transition into a guided trip mode similar to standard rideshare navigation.

The system should:
- Begin full GPS navigation immediately after the first match  
- Provide clear, calm, and friendly voice guidance  
- Verbally announce:
  - upcoming turns  
  - pickup locations  
  - dropoff locations  
  - estimated time and distance  

Voice tone should feel supportive and simple, not overly technical or robotic.

---

Map Behavior

- The map automatically switches to full screen when the first hopper is matched  
- The driver’s route remains unchanged visually (no clutter or unnecessary UI changes)  
- The LCD display should remain clean and focused on navigation only  

When additional hoppers are matched:
- The second (or next) hopper appears on the map as an additional stop  
- A small label appears indicating:
  - “Next Hop: X miles away”  

No disruption to the main navigation flow

---

Additional Hopper Matching Flow

When a new hopper is found during an active trip:

- The driver receives a simple prompt:
  - Accept  
  - Decline  

- The prompt should not interrupt navigation aggressively  
- If accepted:
  - The hopper is added to the route as the next logical stop  
- If declined:
  - The system continues without change  

---

Hopper Experience

Once matched with a driver:

Hoppers should be able to:
- View the driver’s live location on the map  
- Track the driver approaching their pickup point  

---

Communication

Add a built-in chat section between driver and hopper:

- Simple messaging interface  
- Used for:
  - quick coordination  
  - clarifications  
- No unnecessary complexity  

---

System Feel

The experience should feel:
- smooth  
- predictable  
- low-pressure  

Drivers stay focused on driving.  
Hoppers feel informed and connected.  

No part of the system should feel overwhelming or distracting.

---

Core Principle

Navigation and matching should work together quietly in the background, guiding the trip without forcing attention.

Everything should feel like it is naturally unfolding, not being managed.
