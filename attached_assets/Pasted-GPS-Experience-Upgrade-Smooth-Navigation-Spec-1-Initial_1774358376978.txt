GPS Experience Upgrade (Smooth Navigation Spec)

1. Initial Map Load (Fix the “zoomed-out route” problem)

Current issue:
	•	Map loads showing full route → feels static and not like navigation

Required behavior:
	•	On destination set OR when driver goes active:
	•	Briefly calculate full route (background)
	•	Then immediately:

👉 Zoom into driver location (primary focus)
👉 Center map on driver
👉 Tilt slightly (3D effect)

⸻

2. Default Camera State (The “Wow Smooth” Look)

This is the core of what you’re after.

Camera Setup:
	•	Zoom: Close (street-level, not city-level)
	•	Tilt: ~45° (slight 3D perspective)
	•	Bearing: Aligned with driver direction (forward-facing)

Result:
	•	Driver sees:
	•	Their car
	•	The road ahead
	•	Direction of movement

Not the whole trip—just what matters right now

⸻

3. Driver Indicator (Critical for GPS Feel)

Replace static marker with:

Car + Direction Arrow Behavior
	•	Car icon = current position
	•	Arrow = heading direction (live rotation based on movement)

States:
	•	Idle:
	•	Arrow visible but subtle
	•	Moving:
	•	Arrow rotates smoothly with heading
	•	Navigation active:
	•	Arrow always points forward along route

⸻

4. Route Visibility (Don’t Remove—Just De-emphasize)

Keep the full route, BUT:
	•	Primary route:
	•	Highlighted clearly ahead of driver
	•	Remaining route:
	•	Slightly faded or thinner
	•	Completed route:
	•	Fades out behind driver

👉 This keeps context without overwhelming the screen

⸻

5. Smart Camera Movement (This is the magic)

Instead of static map:
	•	Camera follows driver smoothly
	•	Slight forward offset (so driver isn’t dead center)

Positioning:
	•	Driver sits slightly lower on screen
	•	More space shown ahead than behind

⸻

6. Alignment Tool (What you’re actually wanting here)

You don’t need a complex tool—just this:

“Recenter / Align Button”
	•	Tap → snaps camera back to:
	•	Driver position
	•	Correct heading
	•	Proper zoom + tilt

Auto-behavior:
	•	If user drags map:
	•	Pause auto-follow
	•	After X seconds OR button tap:
	•	Re-align smoothly

⸻

7. Transition Into Navigation (Make it feel alive)

Right now it probably just appears.

Instead:

When driver goes active:
	1.	Map loads route (zoomed out briefly)
	2.	Smooth animation:
	•	Zooms in to driver
	•	Tilts into 3D
	•	Rotates to match heading

👉 This 0.5–1 second transition = “wow” effect

⸻

8. Matching Integration (Keep your system intact)

When a hopper is matched:
	•	DO NOT reset camera
	•	DO NOT zoom out

Instead:
	•	Route updates dynamically:
	•	Driver → Pickup → Drop-off → Destination

Camera stays:
	•	Locked on driver
	•	Same zoom
	•	Same tilt

👉 This keeps everything feeling continuous

⸻

9. What NOT to Do (Important)

Avoid:
	•	Constant zoom changes
	•	Snapping camera abruptly
	•	Showing full route all the time
	•	Big UI overlays blocking map
