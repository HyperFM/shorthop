SHORT HOP — PRIVACY POLICY 

Introduction

Short Hop is designed to help people move forward using efficient combinations of walking and shared route-based travel.

User privacy and personal data protection are core principles of the Short Hop platform.

Short Hop collects only the minimum information required to operate the service.
We do not sell personal data, and we do not share user information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Information is used only to operate the platform and provide transportation matching services.

⸻

1. Information We Collect

Short Hop collects limited information necessary to provide safe and functional service.

Account Information

When creating a Short Hop account, users may provide:

• name or display name
• email address
• phone number
• account password

Purpose:

This information allows Short Hop to:

• create and maintain a secure user account
• enable login authentication
• contact users about account activity or ride status
• verify identity when necessary

Account information is never sold or shared with marketing networks.

⸻

Location Information

Short Hop uses location data to enable movement matching between walkers and drivers.

Location information may include:

• current GPS location
• route direction
• destination location

Purpose:

Location data allows the platform to:

• calculate walking routes
• identify drivers already traveling in a similar direction
• match walkers with drivers whose routes overlap
• provide navigation assistance during movement

Location data is used only while the app is actively being used for travel purposes.

Short Hop does not track user movement when the app is inactive unless the user explicitly enables route sharing.

⸻

Daily Route Information (Drivers)

Drivers may optionally provide recurring route information such as:

• common travel routes
• typical commute times
• preferred travel directions

Purpose:

This information allows the system to:

• match walkers with drivers already traveling in the same direction
• reduce unnecessary detours
• increase efficiency of route-based movement

Driver route data is stored only to improve matching efficiency and is not shared publicly.

⸻

Trip Information

During trips, Short Hop may store:

• pickup and drop-off locations
• route traveled
• time and duration of movement
• ride type (Short Hop, Flex Hop, Power Hop)

Purpose:

Trip information is used to:

• calculate ride costs
• maintain ride history
• resolve support issues
• improve route matching algorithms

Trip history is visible only to the user associated with the trip.

⸻

Payment Information

Short Hop may use third-party payment processors to handle financial transactions.

Possible payment information may include:

• payment method identifier
• transaction confirmation
• payment status

Purpose:

Payment data is used only to:

• process ride payments
• issue refunds when necessary
• maintain transaction records

Short Hop does not store full credit card numbers on its own servers.

Payment data is handled through secure payment processing systems.

⸻

Device Information

Short Hop may collect limited technical information such as:

• device type
• operating system version
• app version
• crash reports

Purpose:

This information allows the platform to:

• maintain app stability
• diagnose technical issues
• improve app performance

No personal identity information is derived from device analytics.

⸻

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Short Hop intentionally avoids collecting unnecessary data.

We do not collect:

• social media data
• personal contact lists
• photo libraries
• microphone recordings
• unrelated browsing behavior

Short Hop collects only information necessary for transportation matching and app functionality.

⸻

3. Data Sharing Policy

Short Hop does not sell user data.

Information is not shared with:

• advertising networks
• data brokers
• third-party marketing companies

Limited information may be shared only when necessary for:

• payment processing
• technical infrastructure
• legal compliance

Any service providers used by Short Hop must comply with strict privacy protection requirements.

⸻

4. Data Security

Short Hop takes reasonable steps to protect user data through:

• encrypted connections (HTTPS)
• secure server infrastructure
• limited access controls
• authentication protections

Only authorized system processes can access sensitive information.

⸻

5. User Control

Users maintain control over their data.

Users can:

• update account information
• disable route sharing
• remove saved routes
• delete their account

When an account is deleted, personal data associated with that account will be removed from active systems unless retention is required for legal or financial records.

⸻

6. Location Permissions

Short Hop requests location access only to provide transportation functionality.

Location permissions allow the app to:

• calculate walking directions
• match walkers with drivers traveling nearby
• navigate active trips

Users may disable location permissions at any time through device settings.

Disabling location access may limit core functionality.

⸻

7. Children’s Privacy

Short Hop is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

⸻

8. Policy Updates

Short Hop may update this privacy policy when necessary to reflect changes in the platform or legal requirements.

Users will be notified of significant changes.

⸻

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, users may contact:

Short Hop Support
privacy@shorthop.com

⸻

Privacy Summary

Short Hop is built on three principles:

• minimal data collection
• no data selling
• transparent usage

User information exists only to make movement easier and safer.

SHORT HOP — CUSTOMER SUPPORT, REFUNDS & SAFETY SYSTEM

Short Hop includes an integrated support system designed to ensure fair treatment, transparent pricing, and rapid issue resolution for both riders and drivers.

Support is provided through a combination of automated systems and an in-app AI customer service assistant.

The system is designed to resolve most issues automatically while allowing escalation when necessary.

⸻

1. Hop Guide — AI Customer Support Assistant

Short Hop includes an in-app AI support assistant called Hop Guide.

Hop Guide provides instant help for common issues related to rides, cancellations, payments, and account questions.

Users can access Hop Guide from the Help section inside the Short Hop app.

Typical user questions include:

• “My driver canceled the ride.”
• “I didn’t receive my refund.”
• “Where is my driver?”
• “My ride never started.”
• “I was charged incorrectly.”

Hop Guide can automatically review trip records and provide immediate resolution.

When necessary, the system can escalate the issue for manual review.

⸻

2. Automatic Refund System

Short Hop includes an automated refund system designed to ensure that users are not charged for rides that do not occur.

If a ride is canceled before pickup occurs, the rider automatically receives a refund.

Situations where automatic refunds apply include:

• driver cancels the ride
• driver fails to arrive within the expected pickup window
• ride fails to start due to technical issues
• system errors preventing ride initiation

When these conditions occur, the system automatically triggers a refund through the payment processor.

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment provider but typically completes within 3–7 business days.

Users are notified in the app when a refund has been issued.

⸻

3. Rider Cancellation Policy

Riders may cancel a ride request at any time.

Refund eligibility depends on the timing of the cancellation.

Full Refund

A full refund is issued when the rider cancels before the driver begins traveling toward the pickup location.

Partial Refund

If the driver has already begun traveling toward the pickup location, a small service fee may apply to compensate the driver for time and fuel.

⸻

4. Driver Cancellation Policy

If a driver accepts a ride but cancels before pickup occurs, the rider automatically receives a full refund.

The platform may monitor repeated driver cancellations to maintain network reliability.

Drivers who frequently cancel accepted rides may receive warnings or temporary restrictions.

⸻

5. No-Show Protection

If a rider fails to appear at the pickup location within the designated waiting period, the ride may be marked as a no-show.

In these cases:

• the driver may receive a small compensation fee
• the remaining ride balance is refunded to the rider

This policy helps protect drivers from lost time while maintaining fairness.

⸻

6. Failed Trip Handling

If a trip begins but cannot be completed due to unexpected circumstances, the platform may issue compensation.

Possible situations include:

• vehicle problems
• navigation errors
• emergency interruptions
• system failures

Depending on the situation, the platform may provide:

• partial refunds
• full refunds
• ride credits

These cases may be automatically processed or reviewed through the support system.

⸻

7. Fraud Prevention

Short Hop monitors ride activity to prevent abuse of cancellation and refund policies.

Accounts that repeatedly exploit refund systems may be temporarily restricted or reviewed.

This helps maintain fairness for all participants in the network.

⸻

8. Trip Safety Features

Short Hop includes safety tools designed to protect both riders and drivers.

Safety features may include:

Driver and rider ratings
Trip tracking
Report unsafe behavior
Account blocking tools
Emergency contact options

Users can report safety concerns directly through the app.

Reports are reviewed by the support system to maintain community standards.

⸻

9. AI Support System Behavior (Developer Implementation)

The Hop Guide AI assistant can access specific system data in order to resolve support issues.

The AI may retrieve information such as:

• ride status
• cancellation logs
• payment transaction status
• driver arrival time
• trip history

When a user reports an issue, the AI assistant may perform actions such as:

Checking ride status
Verifying cancellation events
Triggering automatic refunds
Providing trip summaries
Submitting escalation requests

The AI assistant does not access unnecessary personal data.

Its access is limited to information required to resolve ride support issues.

⸻

10. Escalation System

If Hop Guide cannot resolve a problem automatically, the issue may be escalated for manual review.

Escalation may occur when:

• payment disputes occur
• safety incidents are reported
• unusual system behavior is detected
• the user requests further review

⸻

Design Principle

Short Hop is designed around cooperation, fairness, and trust.

The platform prioritizes:

• transparent pricing
• fair cancellation policies
• automatic refunds when rides do not occur
• responsive support systems

Transportation should feel simple, fair, and human.

Short Hop helps people move forward — one hop at a time.

SHORT HOP — AUTOMATED OPERATIONS SYSTEM

Short Hop is designed to operate as a highly automated mobility platform.
Core systems such as identity verification, ride matching, payments, refunds, and customer support are handled automatically through integrated software systems.

The goal of the Short Hop platform is to minimize manual intervention while maintaining safety, fairness, and reliability for users.

⸻

1. Automated Identity Verification

To maintain trust and safety, Short Hop requires identity verification for both riders and drivers.

Verification is completed automatically using identity verification services.

Users may be asked to provide:

• government-issued ID
• selfie verification
• phone number verification
• email confirmation

The system automatically checks:

• ID authenticity
• facial match between selfie and ID
• duplicate accounts
• fraud risk indicators

If verification fails, the account may be restricted until the user provides additional verification.

This process occurs automatically without manual review unless unusual activity is detected.

⸻

2. Automated Driver Verification

Drivers must complete an automated driver onboarding process.

Required information may include:

• driver’s license
• vehicle information
• license plate number
• vehicle photo

The verification system checks:

• license validity
• vehicle registration compatibility
• duplicate driver accounts

Once verified, the driver account becomes active in the Short Hop network.

⸻

3. Automated Route Matching System

Short Hop uses automated route matching to connect walkers with drivers already traveling in the same direction.

The system calculates:

• walker route
• driver route
• route overlap distance
• estimated pickup points

Based on these calculations, the system determines whether the trip qualifies for:

Short Hop (route overlap)
Flex Hop (small detour allowed)
Power Hop (direct ride)

Matching is performed automatically by the platform.

⸻

4. Automated Payment Processing

All ride payments are processed automatically through integrated payment processors.

The system handles:

• ride charges
• cancellation fees
• refunds
• ride credits

Users receive notifications when payments are processed.

Short Hop does not store full credit card numbers on its own servers.

⸻

5. Automated Refund System

The Short Hop system automatically processes refunds when rides do not occur.

Refund triggers include:

• driver cancellation
• driver failure to arrive
• ride start failure
• technical errors

When these events occur, the platform automatically issues a refund through the payment system.

Users are notified once the refund is initiated.

⸻

6. Hop Guide — AI Customer Support System

Short Hop includes an AI customer service assistant called Hop Guide.

Hop Guide is designed to resolve most support requests automatically.

The assistant can:

• check ride status
• verify cancellation events
• process refund requests
• answer account questions
• explain trip charges

Hop Guide reads limited system information such as:

• ride history
• payment status
• trip status

If the AI cannot resolve the issue, the system may escalate the case for review.

⸻

7. Community Trust System

Short Hop maintains safety using a community trust model.

After each trip:

• riders rate drivers
• drivers rate riders

Repeated low ratings or reports may trigger automated account review.

Users can also report unsafe behavior through the app.

⸻

8. Trip Monitoring

During active rides, the system tracks trip progress.

Trip monitoring includes:

• GPS route tracking
• trip duration
• route completion status

If the system detects abnormal trip behavior, it may trigger safety alerts or support checks.

⸻

9. Fraud Detection

Automated fraud detection systems monitor activity across the platform.

The system looks for patterns such as:

• repeated cancellations
• suspicious refund activity
• duplicate accounts
• payment irregularities

Accounts flagged for fraud may be temporarily restricted.

⸻

10. Admin Oversight (Rare Intervention)

Short Hop is designed to operate automatically under normal conditions.

However, a lightweight administrative dashboard may exist for platform oversight.

This dashboard allows the platform operator to review:

• safety reports
• payment disputes
• unusual account activity

Most normal operations are handled automatically without manual intervention.

⸻

Design Goal

The Short Hop platform is designed to operate with minimal human management.

Automation systems handle:

• identity verification
• ride matching
• payments
• refunds
• customer support
• safety monitoring

This allows the platform to scale efficiently while maintaining fairness and reliability.
