ShortHop Update – Growth Features & Viral Expansion System

Goal:
Implement built-in growth mechanics that encourage users to invite friends, stay active, and share ShortHop organically as the Lexington network grows.

These features should create a fun, community-driven environment while helping the platform expand naturally.

---

1. Hop Streak System

Add a “Hop Streak” tracker that rewards users for consecutive rides or activity.

Example notifications:

"You completed 3 hops this week!"

"Your Hop Streak is growing 🛞"

"Keep the streak alive — someone nearby needs a hop."

Display streak information inside the user profile.

Example:

Hop Streak: 5  
Total Hops: 23

Optional rewards for streak milestones:

3 hops  
10 hops  
25 hops  
50 hops

Rewards may include:

• FlexHop trial days  
• special badges  
• recognition in the community

---

2. Referral Rewards System

Add an Invite Friends feature that rewards users for bringing new Hoppers and Drivers into the network.

Inside the app include:

Invite Friends

Allow sharing via:

• text message  
• social media  
• messenger apps  
• email

Example invite text:

"Join me on ShortHop — a new way for people in Lexington to share rides along their routes. Shared routes. Real connections."

Referral rewards examples:

• 1 free FlexHop week  
• ride credit  
• bonus Hop points

Both the inviter and the new user should receive the reward.

---

3. Lexington Leaderboard

Create a local leaderboard to highlight active community members.

Example page:

Lexington Hopper Leaders

1️⃣ Alex – 32 hops  
2️⃣ Jordan – 29 hops  
3️⃣ Sam – 24 hops

Leaderboard categories:

• Most Hops  
• Most Helpful Drivers  
• Community Hoppers

This feature encourages friendly competition and participation.

---

4. Busy Route Notifications

Implement route awareness notifications.

If the system detects active riders along common routes, notify drivers.

Example notifications:

"3 Hoppers are moving along your usual route."

"You’re near a busy route — want to go active?"

"Hoppers are nearby along your route today."

This encourages drivers to activate when demand is present.

---

5. Hop Stories (Community Feed)

Add a simple community feed called:

Hop Stories

Users can share short updates about rides.

Examples:

"First Hop today!"

"Met someone awesome on my route."

"Helped someone get to work this morning."

This builds social proof and strengthens the community.

Standard users:

• can view posts

FlexHop users:

• can post  
• can comment  
• can follow other users

---

6. Achievement Badges

Create badge rewards for participation.

Examples:

🛞 Founding Hopper  
🛞 10 Hop Club  
🛞 100 Hop Club  
🛞 Community Driver  
🛞 Trusted Hopper

Badges should appear on user profiles.

---

7. Shareable Ride Cards

After completing a ride, offer a share option.

Example card text:

"Just completed a ShortHop ride in Lexington 🛞  
Shared routes. Real connections."

Allow users to share this via social media or messaging apps.

This helps spread awareness of the platform.

---

8. Notification Balance

Ensure notifications are helpful but not overwhelming.

Recommended limits:

• no more than 3–5 notifications per day  
• users can disable any notification type in settings

---

9. Goal of These Features

ShortHop should grow primarily through:

• community participation  
• referrals  
• visible activity  
• user achievements

The platform should feel like a movement forming inside the city rather than just a transportation app.
