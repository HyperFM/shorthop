Tagline
Hop, Skip, and a Jump.

Alternate working tagline (optional):

What Short Hop Is (one sentence)

Short Hop connects walkers with drivers who are already headed the same way, helping people save time and money without pressure or full rides.

⸻

App Description (cleaned + tightened)

Short Hop is for those moments when walking almost works.

You’re close, but not close enough.
The bus just passed.
Uber wants $12 for a 5-minute ride.

Short Hop helps you walk first, then advance when someone is already passing your way. Drivers earn small credits for helping along routes they already take. Walkers save money, stress, and time.

No full rides.
No pressure.
Just momentum.

⸻

1. THE PROBLEM (why this exists)

People don’t usually need a ride.
They need help with the last mile.

Common situations:
	•	You’re running late, but the destination is close
	•	Bus routes don’t line up
	•	Uber/Lyft is overpriced for short trips
	•	Walking the whole way takes just a bit too long

Current apps force an all-or-nothing decision.
Short Hop fills the gap.

⸻

2. THE CORE IDEA (the soul)

Walking is the default.
Advancement is the assist.
Full rides are the backup.

Short Hop doesn’t push spending — it optimizes movement.

⸻

3. USER TYPES

A. Walkers
	•	Commuters
	•	Students
	•	City walkers
	•	People mixing walking, transit, and rides

They want:
	•	to save time
	•	to avoid overpaying
	•	to reduce stress

⸻

B. Drivers (Helpers)
	•	Already driving somewhere
	•	Have routine routes (work, school, gym, errands)
	•	Not looking for gig work
	•	Want small offsets (gas, coffee, upkeep)

They want:
	•	flexibility
	•	no obligation
	•	appreciation, not pressure

⸻

4. THE ROUTINE-BASED DRIVER MODEL (KEY UPDATE)

Drivers don’t “work shifts.”

They declare their routine.

Examples:
	•	“Weekdays 8–9am: Home → Downtown”
	•	“Weekdays 5–6pm: Downtown → Home”
	•	“Tue/Thu evenings: Gym → Home”

Short Hop:
	•	knows these routes exist
	•	only suggests hops along those paths
	•	never asks for detours

Drivers can:
	•	toggle routines on/off
	•	ignore requests with zero penalty
	•	help only when it fits naturally

This keeps the system ambient, cooperative, and humane.

7. DRIVER INCENTIVE SYSTEM (non-exploitative)

Drivers earn Credits, not wages.

How it works:
	•	1 mile advanced = 1 credit
	•	Credits redeemable for:
	•	gas
	•	coffee
	•	snacks
	•	car wash
	•	basic vehicle upkeep

Why this matters:
	•	keeps it supplemental
	•	avoids gig-worker classification
	•	feels cooperative, not extractive

Drivers see:
	•	“You helped 2 people today”
	•	“You earned 3 credits”

No leaderboard pressure.
No gamification guilt.

⸻
