SHORT HOP — APP UPDATE INSTRUCTIONS

1. Remove External Ride Options

Remove all options that offer Uber or Lyft.

Short Hop should not send users to another app.

All rides must stay inside the Short Hop system.

⸻

2. Replace “Full Ride” With Power Hop

Change the Full Ride option to Power Hop.

Power Hop is the ultimate ride option inside Short Hop.

It allows users to go directly from their starting point to their destination.

⸻

Power Hop Design

Power Hop should visually stand out.

Design requirements:

• glowing orange and green border around the card
• subtle animated glow effect
• slightly larger card than the other options

Text under Power Hop:

Reach for the Sky

⸻

4. Momentum Suggestions (New Feature)

Add a feature called Momentum Suggestions.

Momentum Suggestions automatically suggest trips based on common movement patterns.

This helps users move quickly without typing destinations.

⸻

Suggested Trips Section

Add a section above the destination search bar.

Section title:

Suggested Trips

Example display:

Home → Work
12 minute walk

Short Hop available in 3 minutes
Flex Hop available now

Main button:

Catch a Hop

This allows users to move forward without typing a destination.

⸻

5. Routine Detection

The app should learn common routes over time.

Examples:

Morning commute
Home → Work

Evening commute
Work → Home

When the user opens the app during these times, show a suggestion.

Example message:

Heading to work?

Then show movement options:

Walk
Short Hop
Flex Hop
Power Hop

⸻

6. Live Route Awareness

When the user starts walking toward a destination, Short Hop should monitor nearby drivers.

If a driver is traveling along the same route, show a notification:

A hop is heading your way.

Button:

Catch a Hop

⸻

7. Visual Motion Indicator

Add animated indicators on the map.

Drivers should appear as small bouncing hop icons moving along streets.

This shows that transportation opportunities are moving through the city in real time.
