1. What Short Hop Is

Short Hop is a movement-first transportation platform that helps people travel from point A to point B using the most efficient combination of walking and rides.

Instead of assuming every trip requires a full ride, Short Hop allows users to move forward in stages.

Users can:

• walk when possible
• advance along routes drivers are already traveling
• request small detours when needed
• take full rides when necessary

The entire journey happens inside one app.

⸻

2. The Problem

Most trips don’t require a full ride.

People usually just need help with the last mile.

Common situations:

• the destination is close but walking takes too long
• bus routes don’t align with where someone needs to go
• rideshare apps charge too much for short trips
• someone is running slightly late

Current transportation apps force an all-or-nothing decision.

Either walk the entire way or pay for a full ride.

Short Hop fills the gap between those two options.

⸻

3. Core Philosophy

Short Hop is built around a simple progression.

1️⃣ Walk when possible
2️⃣ Advance along routes already in motion
3️⃣ Request flexible help if needed
4️⃣ Take a full ride only when necessary

Transportation should support momentum, not replace it.

⸻

4. User Types

Walkers

People moving through a city who want flexible travel.

Examples:

• commuters
• students
• downtown pedestrians
• transit users combining walking and rides

Walkers want:

• to save time
• to avoid expensive rides
• to keep moving without friction

⸻

Drivers (Helpers)

Drivers are not gig workers.

They are simply people already traveling somewhere.

Examples:

• commuting to work
• going to the gym
• running errands
• heading home

Drivers can help someone along their route when it makes sense.

There is no obligation to accept requests.

⸻

5. Routine-Based Driver Model

Drivers do not work shifts.

Instead, they declare routine routes.

Examples:

• Weekdays 8–9 AM: Home → Downtown
• Weekdays 5–6 PM: Downtown → Home
• Tue/Thu evenings: Gym → Home

Short Hop uses these routes to detect possible advances.

The system:

• only suggests hops along declared routes
• avoids unnecessary detours
• allows drivers to toggle routes on/off anytime

This keeps the platform cooperative rather than transactional.

⸻

6. Integrated Navigation System

Short Hop includes a built-in GPS navigation system.

Users never need to leave the app.

Navigation includes:

• real-time map
• walking directions
• voice guidance
• animated movement indicator

While walking, the map displays a small animated walking icon moving along the path.

Voice prompts guide the user step-by-step.

Example:

“Walk straight for 0.3 miles.”
“Turn right at the next intersection.”

During the walk, the system continuously checks for available drivers along the route.

⸻

7. Movement Options

When a user enters a destination, Short Hop calculates the best movement strategy.

The app presents the following options.

⸻

🟢 Walk

Encouraged when the destination is nearby.

Display includes:

• distance
• estimated time
• optional health stats

Navigation features:

• GPS path
• animated walking indicator
• voice guidance

Cost: $0

⸻

🟡 Short Hop (Advance)

Short Hop connects walkers with drivers already traveling along the same path.

Drivers do not change their route.

They simply advance the walker forward.

Example:

Driver route
Home → Downtown

Walker destination
2 miles further along that direction.

Driver advances the walker part of the way.

Cost: $1–$3

Benefits:

• extremely low cost
• minimal driver disruption
• efficient movement

⸻

🟠 Flex Hop (Optional Detours)

Some drivers may enable Flex Hop.

These drivers allow small detours when helpful.

Example:

Driver route
Home → Downtown

Walker destination
1 mile past the route.

Driver prompt:

“Flex Hop request available — continue 0.8 miles past route.”

Driver options:

• Accept detour
• Continue normal route

Drivers can configure:

• maximum detour distance
• maximum extra time
• detours on/off

Pricing adjusts dynamically.

⸻

🔵 Full Ride

If a longer trip is required, Short Hop can provide a complete ride from start to destination.

This option appears when:

• distance is long
• no route matches exist
• the user prefers a direct ride

Full ride pricing adjusts based on distance and demand.

Users can always reach their destination without leaving the app.

⸻

Ride costs:

Short Hop: $1–$3

9. Driver Incentive System — Wheels

Short Hop includes a driver reward system called Wheels.

Wheels reward drivers for helping walkers advance along their route.

This encourages cooperation while keeping participation voluntary.

⸻

How Wheels Are Earned

Drivers earn Wheels based on distance advanced.

Example conversion:

1 mile advanced = 1 Wheel

Examples:

Advance walker 2 miles → 2 Wheels

Advance multiple walkers totaling 3 miles → 3 Wheels

Wheels accumulate in the Driver Dashboard.

⸻

Driver Dashboard

Drivers can view:

• total Wheels earned
• riders helped
• distance advanced
• available rewards

Example display:

“You helped 3 people today.”
“You earned 4 Wheels.”

This keeps the experience positive without competitive pressure.

⸻

10. Reward Redemption System (Launch Version)

At launch, rewards are delivered through digital gift cards.

This allows Short Hop to reward drivers immediately without requiring brand partnerships.

Drivers redeem Wheels for rewards such as:

• coffee
• gas credits
• drive-through meals
• automatic car washes

Example redemption:

Wheels	Reward
5 Wheels	Coffee reward
10 Wheels	Gas credit
15 Wheels	Drive-through meal
20 Wheels	Car wash

Drivers receive a digital code inside the app.

⸻

17. Launch Strategy

Short Hop launches as a small pilot program.

Initial constraints:

• single city
• main roads only
• 1–2 mile hop limits
• commute hours first

The goal is validating demand before scaling.

⸻

18. Strategic Position

Traditional ride apps focus on full rides.

Short Hop focuses on partial movement.

Instead of competing directly, Short Hop fills the gap between walking and rides.

⸻

19. The Ethos

Short Hop is not about disruption.

It is about momentum.

Walking is respected.
Assistance is optional.
Movement should feel natural.

Short Hop simply helps people get where they’re already going.

⸻

20. Design Principle

Short Hop assumes capability.

The platform provides a clean, unified experience where users move from A to B without leaving the app.

Transportation should feel simple, human, and cooperative.
